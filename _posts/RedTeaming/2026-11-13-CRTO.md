    <h2>CRTO — Certified Red Team Operator</h2>

  <p>
    CRTO is Zero Point Security's red team certification focused on operating Cobalt Strike
    in a realistic adversary simulation environment. The course covers the full attack chain
    from an OPSEC-first perspective: defense evasion, lateral movement, Kerberos attacks,
    MSSQL abuse, ADCS, forest trusts, and pivoting.
  </p>

  <p>
    The exam is a 24-hour hands-on operation against a realistic Active Directory environment.
    The objective is simple: write a file to fileserver on a target machine
    deep inside a segmented network. No CTF flags, no walkthroughs — just you,
    Cobalt Strike and a fully monitored enterprise environment scoring every OPSEC
    decision you make.

    This was a challenging exam. On the first try the environment got stuck -> failed. Second attempt I took a wrong path and got stuck :D. And I keep wondering: how the Defender flagged my beacon, i checked everything on the Threatcheck-tool that everything was fine and no fingerprints were seen.
  </p>

  <p>
    Third attempt. Score: <strong>91/100</strong>. Updating soon....
  </p>
